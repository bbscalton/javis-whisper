How javis behaves – like a friend, not a commentator

Listens to the whole back-and-forth
It doesn’t jump on the first sentence. It lets a few turns land – both sides – before it even thinks about coaching.
Works out the topic first
It keeps a running picture of what you’re talking about and what’s still open, and only speaks once it’s sure it understands.
Knows when you’re talking to it
“Whisper, what’s the time?” (also Javis / Jarvis) is a request – you get the answer right away. “What’s the time?” asked between you and someone else is just conversation.
Learns who you are
Your voice is the close one: louder against the room, more bass, less echo, and it matches your recent turns. javis keeps learning your voice from your “Whisper, …” requests. Optional: Teach javis my voice.
Waits when unsure
If it can’t tell who spoke, it keeps listening instead of guessing or interrupting.
Ignores background talk
Conversations around you that don’t involve you are waited out.
Only whispers when you’re in it
Once you reply to someone on the same topic, javis knows you’re part of that conversation – then, at a natural pause after they finish, it gives you one short heads-up that fits your pre-brief.
Short and human
Warm, plain words, leads with the answer, one sentence – or nothing at all.

1 · Install

  1. Tap Download for Android on the phone, open the file, allow Install unknown apps for your browser when asked.
  2. Open Javis Whisper. When you press START the first time, allow Microphone, Nearby devices (Bluetooth) and Notifications.
  3. Samsung: Settings → Apps → Javis Whisper → Battery → Unrestricted, so it keeps listening with the screen off.

2 · Pair with PC01 (once – this also loads the xAI key)

  1. On PC01 open the admin page (link in C:\javis-whisper\README-admin.txt) and click New pairing code. Codes last 10 minutes and work once.
  2. In the app tap Settings, scroll to PC01, check Server URL https://whisper.neuereatec.org, type the 6‑digit code and tap Pair once.
  3. You should see “Paired ✔ · xAI key received ✔”. No key typing needed. If it fails, the app shows the real reason (expired/used code, no internet, revoked).
  4. Alternative: paste your own xAI key in Settings → Save & test key.
  5. After pairing the phone talks to xAI directly (speech-to-text, Grok, Ara voice); the key is stored encrypted on the phone.

3 · Connect the earbuds

  1. Pair the earbuds in Android Bluetooth settings as normal and make sure Calls is enabled for them.
  2. Wear the earbud(s). The app uses the earbud microphone in “call” mode (SCO) – the status line shows Earbuds (SCO) · mic earbud.
  3. If the earbuds drop out, Javis reconnects automatically when they come back.

4 · Pre-brief & go

  1. Pick a mode, then type a short pre-brief: who you are meeting and the topic (e.g. “GBTI loan officer, crane truck financing”).
  2. Press START listening. Put the phone face‑up on the table or in a shirt pocket – the phone mic hears the other person (“Them”), your earbud hears you (“Me”).
  3. Talk normally. javis listens first: in Coach mode expect silence for the first turns while it works out the topic. When it does speak, it’s one short line in Ara’s voice at a natural pause after the other person finishes. It keeps listening while it whispers.
  4. Need something now? Start with the wake word: “Whisper, what’s the time?”, “Javis, how do you say thank you in Spanish?”, “Jarvis, who won yesterday?” – it answers in about 2 seconds.
  5. Press STOP (or Stop in the notification). The session summary is saved and makes the next session smarter.

5 · Let javis learn your voice

  1. Nothing to do: every time you say “Whisper, …” javis learns a bit more about how your voice sounds close to the earbud.
  2. Optional head start: press START, then Settings → Teach javis my voice and say one normal sentence with the earbud in while others are quiet.
  3. Each line in the live log shows a near score – high = you, low = someone further away, in between = javis isn’t sure and waits.

Modes

Coach
Listens first, then – only when you’re part of the conversation – gives one heads-up: a corrected fact, an answer you were asked, or a sharp point. Otherwise it stays quiet.
Translate
Detects their language (set up to 3 in Settings, e.g. es‑ES, pt‑BR, fr‑FR), whispers the English meaning and a short reply you can say in their language.
Street
Walking around: when someone further away asks you something, you get a quick, friendly answer to repeat.

Settings

  • Whisper volume – default is quiet (35%). Also use the phone’s volume buttons during a session.
  • Mic sensitivity Low / Normal / High – raise it if quiet speakers are missed, lower it in noisy places. Watch the two level meters.
  • Use phone mic for the other person – on = Me/Them labels (recommended). Off = earbud only.
  • Speak whispers – off = silent mode, read whispers on screen.
  • Model / Voice – default grok-4.20-non-reasoning (fastest) and ara.

Privacy

  • No audio is ever saved – not on the phone, not on PC01. Sound is held in memory for one sentence, transcribed, and thrown away.
  • Only text (transcript + whispers, timestamped) is stored, on your phone. Review it in Review history, export it as JSON. Optional text backup to your PC01.
  • No API keys are inside the app – you enter your own, it stays encrypted on the phone. Audio is sent to xAI only to transcribe it.
  • Tell people when you are using an AI assistant where the law or courtesy requires it.

Troubleshooting

  • “Key test failed” – check the key, internet, and that your xAI plan has credit and voice access.
  • Status shows “Phone (no earbuds)” – reconnect the earbuds in Bluetooth settings and make sure “Calls” audio is on; the app picks them up automatically.
  • Coach says nothing? That’s often on purpose: it waits until you’ve replied on topic and a few turns have landed. The log shows why (“listening: …”, “background”, “unsure”). Use “Whisper, …” for an instant answer.
  • Silent after START? Look at the bar above START: it shows the status and the last line heard. Talk – the two meters should move and “Them: …” lines appear. “mic is silent” = permissions/earbuds; “(javis stayed quiet)” = nothing useful to add (try asking a factual question).
  • Meters move but nothing is transcribed – raise Mic sensitivity; speak within 1 m of the phone.
  • Whispers too loud / others hear them – lower Whisper volume; use one earbud.
  • It stops when the screen is off – set Battery to Unrestricted (step 1.3).
  • Hears its own whisper – it ignores echoes automatically; if it still repeats, lower the volume.
  • No internet = no whispers (xAI is in the cloud); history stays on the phone.

0.1.5 – remembers people, reads the room

⬇ Download 0.1.5

Available – built and published, not yet tried on a phone. Everything from 0.1.4 stays the same (voice profile, listening before coaching, waiting when unsure, “Whisper, …”, only coaching once you’re in the conversation).

Reads the room
Works out if you’re in a negotiation, sale, meeting, call, casual chat, family time or on the street – from your pre-brief and what’s said – and coaches to match: quick nudges on numbers in a negotiation, nearly silent at family dinner. Tap the 🧭 Setting chip to choose it yourself.
Remembers people
Learns names from your pre-brief (“Meeting John Persaud about the loan”) and introductions (“Hi, this is Marcus”). Next time it can remind you: “Last time he wanted delivery by Friday.” A voice that sounds familiar only counts once the name agrees.
Knows your goal
Picks your goal out of the pre-brief and keeps you on it: “You wanted 6% – they haven’t said a rate yet.”
Recap after
When a conversation ends you get a short note – topic, who promised what, what’s still open – as a notification and on the People screen. Ask any time: “Whisper, what did I promise?”
People screen
See who javis remembers and your past conversations with them. Edit, merge two entries, forget a voice, or delete someone completely.
Gets the joke, hears doubt
Never “corrects” a joke or sarcasm. Notices “um… maybe… I guess” and can suggest a gentle push.
Its own rhythm
Waits a longer beat for slow talkers before deciding to whisper. Says “Not sure – ask them” instead of guessing.
Local smarts
Guyana dollars, local places, and Guyanese Creolese understood (“wha yuh seh”, “just now”).
Warmer Ara
Ara speaks a touch slower and drops quieter while you’re talking.
Private
People and notes are text only, kept on your phone, and you can delete them. Never audio. (Extra database encryption is still to come.)

0.1.6 – hears how it’s said

⬇ Download 0.1.6

Available – built and published, not yet tried on a phone. Everything from 0.1.4 stays the same (voice profile, listening before coaching, waiting when unsure, “Whisper, …”, only coaching once you’re in the conversation).

Includes everything in 0.1.5.

Tone from the voice
javis compares each person with how they usually sound – pitch, loudness, pace, a rise at the end – and notices “louder”, “faster”, “higher than usual”. It treats these as hints, not mind-reading: “He’s speeding up – get to the number.”
Talking over each other
When two people talk at once, javis holds its whisper. If you got cut off and your point mattered: “Finish your point about the deposit.”
Sharper voice recognition
Your voice profile now also learns your typical pitch and range, which helps tell you from others and tell two other people apart. (Better sound cues, not an AI voiceprint model.)
The honest blind spot
javis can’t see faces or body language. It uses timing instead – a long pause before an answer, or an instant reply – and never pretends to see.

0.1.7 – a friend who helps you decide

⬇ Download 0.1.7

Available – built and published, not yet tried on a phone. Includes everything in 0.1.6. Made from Calton’s first real-world test: “it kept asking me questions and took too long to catch on.”

A friend in your ear helps you make good decisions in the conversation around you.

Suggestions, not questions
Every whisper is something you can use: a recommendation (“Take the 5 kVA”), a red flag (“No receipt, no warranty – get it written”), a better option (“Offer G$260k cash, delivery included”), what to say next, or a key fact. It never asks you anything back. If it isn’t sure, it stays quiet.
Catches on fast
Can help after the first exchange instead of waiting for several turns. Uses your pre-brief from the very first moment and guesses the topic from the first line.
Not shy any more
Shorter gaps between whispers (about 10–12 seconds in most settings) and a lower bar for speaking up when it has something useful.
Still a good listener
Still waits for the other person to finish, still ignores background talk you’re not part of, and “Whisper, …” still answers right away.
Leaner thinking
Half-size instructions to Grok, so each decision costs less; about 1.1–1.4 s per decision.

0.1.8 – Send to PC

⬇ Download 0.1.8

Available – built and published, not yet tried on a phone. Includes everything in 0.1.7.

Send to PC
Tap ⇪ Send to PC on the main screen and pick Last session, Today or Last 24 hours. javis sends the transcripts, its whispers, why it stayed quiet, voice scores and timings to your paired PC, then shows the saved file name.
Text only
Never audio. It uses this phone’s pairing, so only your paired PC can receive it.
A copy on the phone
Each send also saves a copy in Downloads/JavisWhisper, so it can be copied over USB, or sent later if the PC is off.
A fuller log
The app now keeps its log (text only, about 3 MB) so a review can see what javis heard, decided and said.
